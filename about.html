Who We Are

Kapp Nordic is a small consultancy built on Scandinavian simplicity and practical problem-solving. We help organisations work smarter by improving processes, applying the right technology, and increasing predictability.

Our Logo & Identity

Our logo combines two runic forms representing K and N—a nod to our Nordic roots and our belief in clarity, structure, and purposeful design. The darker blue symbolises stability and trust; the lighter blue represents forward movement, creativity, and progress.
